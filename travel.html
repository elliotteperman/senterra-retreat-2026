Once you land: two ways to the Abbey Inn

Recommended

Option A: Train + hotel shuttle

  1. Get yourself to Grand Central Terminal. From JFK: AirTrain + LIRR or subway. From EWR: Newark Airport Express. From LGA: taxi/rideshare.
  2. Take Metro-North's Hudson Line from Grand Central to Peekskill: roughly hourly on weekdays, ~60–75 min.
  3. The Abbey Inn offers a free shuttle to and from Peekskill station, but it must be booked in advance. Email Megan with your train's arrival time to reserve a seat. The shuttle doesn't run between 5:00 and 7:00 PM; outside those hours, local taxis and rideshare are available.

Option B: Uber/Lyft/car service direct to the hotel

Faster door-to-door but pricier: rough estimate $120–180+ from JFK/EWR/LGA depending on traffic and surge (less from HPN). Going by car makes the most sense for late or off-hour arrivals, when trains are infrequent and there's no traffic.

Renting a car? The Abbey Inn offers free valet parking for the duration of our event.

Check-out is at 11:30 AM on Friday for those not staying Friday night. Please pack up and check out either before breakfast or during the 11:00–11:30 AM break. Luggage storage is available at the front desk.
