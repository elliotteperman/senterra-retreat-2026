12:15 pmLightning talks: Come find me if you want to talk about...45 minMultiple

What it covers

This session will be a series of 5-minute talks, each an invitation for more discussion on a topic chosen by the speaker. There will be no Q&A after each talk, so conversations continue over the rest of the retreat.

Who it's for

All attendees, especially anyone looking for new ideas or people to connect with.

What you'll get out of it

Attendees will get a quick tour of topics members care about, and will know which speakers to find for follow-up conversations.

Talks include

  • Ultra processed Foods: Opportunity and Risk Meghan O'Brien Lowery

    AB 1264 is the first law in the country to legally define "ultra-processed food," and whoever shapes that definition by 2028 decides whether alt-protein gets grouped with the problem or positioned as the solution. The catch is that our natural allies are split — groups focused on banning processed meat don't always think to protect novel proteins, and if we don't align now, regulators may see "no consensus" and default to the broadest, most restrictive definition.

  • Why active grantmaking will be more important in the future Joey Savoie

    The animal movement may soon receive more philanthropic capital than ever before. If so, most established organisations are likely to become well-funded, creating an absorption problem: there will be more money than strong existing opportunities to spend it on. In that world, conventional grantmaking, which funds what already exists, will offer diminishing returns. Therefore, the most cost-effective use of each additional dollar is likely through active grantmaking, where funders identify gaps, scope out new opportunities and help bring them into existence.

  • What is the Senterra Steering Committee Shannon Campion

    What is the Senterra Steering Committee, what does it do, and what is involved in joining the Steering Committee for 2027?

  • Unlocking More Climate Capital for Food Systems Max Elder

    I’ll share three efforts FSI is pursuing to help unlock more philanthropic capital for food systems: mapping coordination opportunities across the broader field, bringing meat and dairy funders together, and building a coordinated funding vehicle for sustainable proteins. Come find me afterward if you’re interested in food systems philanthropy, funder coordination, or working together to bring more climate capital into food and sustainable proteins.

  • How Elections Are Won And Lost Chris Conrad

    Improve your mental model of how to think about elections, with some preliminary implications for our movement.

  • We Need More Policy Researchers Gage Weston

    The movement has a growing number of policy advocates, people working in government, and funding for policy work, but almost no one is doing the deep research required to generate the concrete, tractable policy ideas needed to turn that effort into impact. This talk argues that funding policy research—whether through think tanks or by funding researchers inside advocacy organizations—is one of the most neglected, cost-effective levers to create change for farm animals.
